# Sebetsa Product Scope

Sebetsa is an enterprise workforce and operations platform built specifically for commercial cleaning and facilities-management businesses.

## Core operating model

**Cleaning company → clients → contracts → sites → teams → employees → shifts → work → evidence → service performance**

## Workforce

- Employee records
- Employment status
- Teams and supervisors
- Site assignments
- Scheduling and availability
- Attendance and worked hours
- Leave and approvals
- Workforce documents
- Training and skills
- Performance management

## Cleaning operations

- Client and contract management
- Multi-site operations
- Site staffing
- Cleaning duties and task execution
- Checklists and evidence
- Incidents and corrective actions
- Compliance and safety
- Service-level management
- Operational reporting
- Asset and equipment management
- Cleaning inventory and consumables
- Procurement
- Workforce communications

## Enterprise direction

Sebetsa is designed for cleaning enterprises operating across multiple clients, contracts, branches, regions and service sites.

The product priority is operational control: know **who is working, where they are assigned, what work is required, what was completed, what went wrong, who approved it, and how the contract is performing**.

## Product boundary

The application is a cleaning and facilities operations system. Its navigation, data model, workflows, reports, terminology and future development must remain aligned to that operating model.
